# Preparing and deriving references

Load only when existing references need cropping, cleanup, look transfer, local repair or derivation from footage. Reuse suitable assets directly. Keep originals, derived versions and the property each derivative proves in the existing asset ledger.

## Clean plates and targeted crops

Remove incidental clutter only when it interferes with the intended shot: duplicate hero-like cars, confusing extra props or unwanted text. Preserve accepted landmarks and required factual detail. A cleaned plate is a new version; inspect edges, reflections, shadows and repaired geometry before reuse. Distinguish an empty environment plate from a populated composition anchor.

Crop a reliable face or product feature from an accepted source when it supplies missing evidence at sufficient resolution. Compare it with canon first; a tiny face crop must not replace a stronger canonical portrait simply because it appears on the outfit sheet. Avoid treating an enlarged crop as new captured detail. A logo crop controls the mark; use a separate geometry view for the wheel rim/spokes, dashboard, shell or surrounding construction.

For visible text, decide what the audience must read. Prepare exact important copy as controlled artwork; nonessential fine print can be intentionally defocused or simplified when the brief permits it. Check the delivered viewing size. Blur must not hide required product information or story evidence.

## Separate look from structure and performance

Explore palette/mood with inexpensive rough frames before applying the accepted look to detailed assets, when exploration is actually needed. Assign the look reference to color/light character, and the layout source to geography. Protect required skin/product colors and motivated lighting; one shared grade does not mean identical exposure everywhere.

If identity conditioning repeatedly weakens the needed pose or performance, consider a scene/performance base followed by a scoped identity repair. Compare its total preparation, editing and generation cost with direct reference-based work. Match face angle, expression, age, light and occlusion; inspect outside the edit region as well as the repaired face. This is a conditional decomposition, not a mandatory face-swap workflow or permission to recast. Preserve the accepted identity authority throughout.

## Derive stills from inspected footage

Prefer useful existing footage, real references or controlled 3D views before purchasing video solely to obtain stills. A short static-coverage clip can supply candidate angles if justified, but adjacent frames are not consistent by definition.

For every selected still, save `source clip/version | timestamp or frame and frame rate | crop/transform | role | inspected properties | unresolved details`. Compare selected frames against canon and each other: cabin arrangement, seat count, wheel construction, mirrors, landmarks, perspective, lettering and proportions as relevant. Reject drifted frames rather than converting their mistakes into new authority. Check compression, blur and source resolution for the planned reuse.

Keep accepted clean single-view inputs separate from human review boards. Confirm that the writer and the execution tool can access the same intended version. Preserve exact tool aliases in a manifest, but verify their actual bindings; similar names, changed filenames and an asset pack's title do not prove that every prompt reference is present.
