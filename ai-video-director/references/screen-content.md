# Content inside a screen

Load for a TV, phone, monitor or projection whose displayed content, timing or exactness matters. A decorative unreadable screen does not need a full media record.

## Separate the two timelines

Record the outer shot independently from the inner media:

- Outer: shot/version, physical display size and placement, visible surface/corners/bezel, camera movement, foreground occlusion and reflections.
- Inner: source file/version, source in/out, normal or changed playback speed, outer-shot start offset, fit/crop, channel changes and required legibility.
- Sound: inner clip audio included, muted or replaced; perspective/level and relationship to dialogue, music and other ambience. Route each source once.

Map source time to shot time explicitly. A six-second source inside an eight-second shot leaves two seconds to resolve through a planned hold, loop, trim, extension or other edit. Sources need not universally equal the outer duration. Avoid accidental freezes, invented continuation and duplicate audio. Name the allowed content at each channel change; vague requests for “various channels” may introduce unintended imagery or text.

## Choose fidelity before the route

If exact source pixels, interface states, lettering or frame timing must survive, use a controlled composite when feasible: track/corner-pin the screen, mask foreground objects, preserve the bezel and match light, reflections, blur and color. Inspect tracking through occlusion and reacquisition. Warping underlying geometry can make a replacement infeasible; evaluate the actual plate before promising repair.

Generated integration is a candidate for flexible creative content only when the selected mode supports the needed inputs and a bounded test can assess fidelity. A video reference may guide motion or style instead of copying its pixels; verify its actual role. Do not silently relax exact-content requirements.

For a transition from displayed footage into a full scene, establish the receiving composition/version first using [continuity](continuity.md#per-shot-transition). Define the boundary where the display ceases to be an object and the new scene fills the frame; match motion, timing and sound as well as the image.

## Accept the assembled result

Review the entire outer shot: stable display size and geometry, correct crop and playback map, content constrained to the surface, proper foreground overlap, required text legibility and intentional audio. Reject an unintended expansion of the screen into the full image. Inspect channel-change and scene-transition frames, then listen to the mix. Stills cannot certify playback or sync. Use the chosen [editing adapter](tool-routing.md) only when executing that route.
