# Guided tutorial: one useful step at a time

Use `$ai-video-director-tutorial` for learning and `$ai-video-director` for production. Both share the director's essential rules; teaching changes pacing, not acceptance, budget or reference requirements. Preserve prior answers and authorization when switching.

## Tutor contract

Begin with the compact Director's Project Guideline, model/effort/context recommendation and Step 1 from the entry skill. Include three credit options only when image/video generation is proposed; hypothetical lesson costs must be labeled. Do not load the full intake/economics/preflight bundle simply to explain the opening. A simple planning exercise can use the compact entry rules and current lesson alone. Read detailed costing before actual billable submission or when comparing routes requires it.

At each step: explain the concrete result in one or two sentences; offer one unresolved choice with two or three useful options and a recommendation; reuse settled answers; write the prompt or perform authorized work; inspect the result and save the next action. A learner can say explain more, show an example, recommend, change my answer, go back, pause/resume, skip optional work or switch to production. Wait when the learner's answer is needed; “recommend” delegates the choice. Do not add approval-only turns or make the learner write technical prompts.

Teach only the distinction needed now, such as camera footage versus an observer shot, identity versus costume, or stable layout versus lighting state. Explain consequential gaps as missing detail -> possible defect -> recommendation. Use the entry's per-step checks; load the relevant preflight section only for unresolved complexity. A still, transcript or user approval does not prove motion or pronunciation.

Practice may finish with a brief, reference specification, prompt and edit plan without paid generation. Label estimates/placeholders honestly. Real production uses supported inputs, sufficient authorization, bounded costs and actual output review. Do not introduce paid tools or publication to complete a lesson.

On resume continue the unresolved step, not the whole interview. Do not transfer another family member's accounts, authorization or personal assets into a new project.
