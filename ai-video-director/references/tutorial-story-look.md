# Tutorial steps 1–3: brief, performance and references

Read only the current step. Use the shared tutor contract; these prompts are choices to adapt, not a questionnaire to complete.

## Step 1: Brief and reference check

**Teach:** the brief describes the viewer's experience, not just the objects in the frame. A woman holding a camera and footage recorded by that camera are different shots.

**Guide:** extract purpose, audience, duration, aspect, viewpoint, must-preserve details and existing assets. Resolve only the gaps that change preparation. After the idea is known, establish planning practice versus a real deliverable and tools/access as needed; existing answers take priority over new menus.

**Choices when relevant:** own-camera POV / observer camera / mixed edited coverage. Or existing footage / new imagery / a combination. Use only the question needed now, not every category.

**References:** inventory what exists. Recommend real location photos for accurate architecture/signs and walking footage for geography/motion; multi-angle identity for a recurring person; exact product photos/dimensions and mechanism evidence for a prop action; source audio for a name or voice requirement. Explain the exact missing view or detail and why it matters. Research suitable public facts; ask the user for their own specific images/videos/prop references when needed. Follow [visual development](visual-development.md#reference-acquisition-before-dependent-prompts).

**Small result:** a few-line brief and a reference list marked available, to acquire, not needed or unresolved. Explain which missing item affects the next step. Do not generate an accurate-location hero while its factual references remain absent.

## Step 2: Story, dialogue and background sound

**Teach:** short videos need enough time for actions, camera turns, words and pauses. Sound can carry information without adding another shot.

**Guide:** draft a beginning, main action and ending using [story](story.md). Offer spoken dialogue, voiceover or no speech only when unresolved. Draft the words yourself, preserve mandatory copy, and time an actual sample before dependent lip-sync work. For foreign/place names, follow the reliable pronunciation and listening checks in [audio](audio-captions.md).

**Character performance, only if useful:** use [the compact performance record](character-performance.md#record-compact-reusable-and-scene-specific). Explain that a face establishes appearance while speech, gestures and pauses establish behavior. Offer one relevant choice, such as conversational / restrained / energetic delivery, with a recommendation; reuse an already chosen style. Write the few-line record for the learner and apply only the scene-relevant slice. Do not require biography or generate a voice for existing recorded speech.

**Music choice when unresolved:** natural ambience only / quiet instrumental bed / music-led edit. Recommend based on the viewer experience and any existing instructions. Explain that music should have a purpose and must leave speech intelligible. A music reference establishes taste, not permission to use that recording.

**Small result:** a short beat plan and cue list showing dialogue, ambience, music or intentional no-music. In planning practice, timing estimates remain estimates. For production, check natural delivery, pronunciation, music cues, speech masking and a viable listening/lip-sync review route.

## Step 3: Choose the look and prepare references

**Teach:** a style image shows appearance; a character sheet records identity; an environment reference records space. One does not prove the others.

**Guide:** offer a relevant style choice only if missing, then produce or specify one representative frame. Reuse accepted identity and wardrobe. For character sheets, follow the skill's front, both profiles, back and relevant three-quarter/head/detail coverage; keep clean individual model inputs separate from the human review board. Verify product shape/operation and layout sources rather than inventing facts.

**Small result:** a reference pack or, in planning practice, explicit reference specifications and prompts. Review facial consistency, costume asymmetry, grip/contact, readable signs, materials and night/day exposure. Label generated unseen angles as interpretations. Avoid requiring a character sheet for a landscape montage without a recurring person.

### Optional character/scene consistency exercise

Load only when the learner is creating or revising recurring assets. Use [character changes](character-performance.md#change-permanent-change-decision-tree) or [scene creation](visual-development.md#scene-creation-record-and-sequence), not both unless both are active. Explain one distinction: “A red jacket is a costume variant; a permanent haircut is a new appearance state of the same person.” Let the learner choose a temporary shot change or an ongoing change if that scope is unresolved, then update the record and identify affected shots. Keep the old version for flashbacks. For scenes, compare a night-light variant with moving a permanent stall; preview only the affected coverage.

For conflicting references, show one relevant [role example](prompt-recipes.md#visibility-and-reference-filter), have the learner identify what each reference should control, and write the actual role declaration. Keep your required angle coverage while explaining why the model may receive fewer selected files than the review board. These are optional explanations inside the active step, not compulsory extra lessons.

### Optional action or reference exercise

Use only the exercise relevant to the learner's scene: identify an exact control on a clean and marked reference with [precision actions](precision-actions.md); separate a hero from a population board with [crowds and scale](crowds-and-scale.md); or check a source frame/crop against canon with [reference preparation](reference-preparation.md). Explain what the reference proves and one missing property. For a reveal such as a rollover, identify the needed underside view. Keep the result to the selected reference/role and next check, not an extra full lesson or paid comparison.
