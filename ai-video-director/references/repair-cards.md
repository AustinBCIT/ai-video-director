# Diagnostic repair cards

Load only after an observed defect or when a known high-risk failure needs a bounded test. Read the matching card, not the whole catalog. A symptom suggests several causes; do not claim that a missing phrase is certainly the cause. A card does not authorize another paid attempt.

## One repair cycle

Record `shot/version | observed defect and time/frame | evidence/reviewer | likely cause(s) | one chosen intervention | preserved passing criteria | verification | quote/attempt cap | outcome`. Inspect the actual request and source assets first. Prefer the smallest viable edit, reference correction or controlled-source change. Test one variable where possible; document coupled changes when inseparable. Stop at acceptance or the agreed cap. Repeated same failure requires a diagnosis/route change, not stronger adjectives and unlimited retries. Unknown review capability remains unverified.

## Identity or performance drift

- **Inspect:** accepted versus submitted identity versions, face crop/resolution, outfit-source face, conflicting references, mirror state; for acting, baseline versus scene direction and actual voice source.
- **Possible causes:** wrong asset/role, insufficient view evidence, source already drifted, overloaded action, new styling inadvertently interpreted as recasting, performance default overriding the scene.
- **Intervention:** correct roles and changed/preserved fields; use the appropriate accepted face/angle; scope the edit to the failed property. Use [character change rules](character-performance.md#change-permanent-change-decision-tree) to distinguish a deliberate new state from a failed output.
- **Pass:** intended identity/age/asymmetry and costume remain consistent at required views and during motion; scene-appropriate voice/gaze/gesture. Preserve previously passing geography and sound. If controllability is insufficient, propose controlled assets/performance instead of promising another prompt will lock it.

## Wrong camera, turn, grip or travel

- **Inspect:** recording source, lens path/target, body/head/gaze, subject versus screen left/right, occupied hands, mirrored inputs and the start image.
- **Possible causes:** observer composition reused for own-camera footage, conflated head/lens rotation, ambiguous left, impossible reach/contact, unsupported device mechanism.
- **Intervention:** fix the direction record and any incompatible hero frame; preview the move cheaply. Preserve usable character/world assets. Do not flip the entire shot to fix direction when text or asymmetry would reverse.
- **Pass:** start, transition and end match intended lens ownership, travel, contact and reverse geography through full motion; exact device operation has evidence. A pretty observer shot does not pass a POV brief.

## Scene reset, drifting signs or background warping

- **Inspect:** fixed landmarks before/after occlusion and turns; distinguish expected parallax from relocation, letter change or reset. Compare source geometry, forward/reverse references and actual control inputs.
- **Possible causes:** missing reverse coverage, conflicting plates, text generated afresh, a single still expected to control a long camera move, enhancement changing a stable render.
- **Intervention:** supply missing consistent coverage, simplify within accepted scope, use controlled camera/background passes, or track an isolated sign when the underlying surface is stable. Do not use a cosmetic grade to hide changing geography or insert an unapproved cut into a continuous take.
- **Pass:** required landmarks, sign shapes/text and occlusion order remain consistent throughout. Large changing surfaces usually need source repair. Use [Blender](tools-blender.md) or the selected editor only when that repair is actually needed.

## Artificial skin, fabric, lighting or atmosphere

- **Inspect:** reference versus scene treatment, actual material response, light direction/exposure, over-sharpening/smoothing, visible scale, steam origin and motion.
- **Possible causes:** beauty/style preset overriding reality, baked reference lighting, incompatible cinematic/device cues, detail demanded beyond image resolution, blanket haze suppressing cooking steam.
- **Intervention:** remove the conflicting preset, clarify motivated light/material behavior, correct source/render settings or perform a selective edit. Preserve identity marks and age; do not treat universal matte skin or added grain as a realism cure.
- **Pass:** believable relevant detail and motivated exposure at viewing size; consistent material/light response during motion. Check that the repair did not change anatomy, costume color or signs.

## Pronunciation, lip-sync, timing or mix failure

- **Inspect:** listen to the actual critical word and approved audio, then review mouth/audio together; time turns, speech, breaths and pauses; isolate dialogue/music/ambience.
- **Possible causes:** incorrect source pronunciation, unbound audio input, insufficient runtime, occluded mouth, generated extra audio, music masking or bad edit alignment.
- **Intervention:** obtain an accurate spoken sample; bind it only through supported controls; retime or revise unlocked copy/coverage, repair off-camera speech or use a verified lip-sync route. Do not correct speech by changing captions alone. A closure map can aid observation but does not replace listening or language expertise.
- **Pass:** critical pronunciation, natural pacing, mouth/audio timing and intelligibility through the whole relevant passage and final mix. A transcript or waveform alone cannot pass it.

## Cost or job-state mismatch

- **Inspect:** submitted settings/count, quote unit, selected model/operation, job IDs, pending reservations and observed debit.
- **Intervention:** reconcile job state and quote before any duplicate submission; preserve reservations for ambiguous jobs. Consult only the applicable [credit accounting](efficiency.md#three-generation-credit-options) section.
- **Pass:** correct result/job identity, bounded next charge and remaining authorization; unknown actual charges labeled unknown. Stop when a cap is reached.

For a recurring defect, use [comparative tests](comparative-tests.md) only when comparing interventions is justified and within scope. Do not turn every repair into a multi-model experiment.
