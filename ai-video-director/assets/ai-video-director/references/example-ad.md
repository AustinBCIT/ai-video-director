# Worked example: a 20-second folding-desk ad

Fictional planning example. The product facts, assets and offer must come from the user. No performance claim, price or generation cost below is an observed result.

## Brief

- Purpose: demonstrate a desk folding away to free space.
- Audience/platform: small-apartment residents, vertical social placement.
- Runtime: 20 seconds.
- Complexity: Standard, with extra product-accuracy attention.
- Tools: GPT reasoning, Higgsfield, Blender and Resolve Free allowed; After Effects excluded.
- Style: warm believable apartment, readable movement, restrained type.
- Deliverable now: storyboard/shot plan and generation handoff.
- Budget: quote before paid production; no standing spend authorization.

## Mandatory versus optional

Required before depicting the mechanism as real: accepted product photos/dimensions and evidence of how it folds. Required before a paid job: supported configuration and adequate authorization. Required before publishing: exact CTA/offer and rights for actual selected assets.

Optional: music, a detailed character sheet, premium motion graphics and current trend research. There is no visible presenter, so do not create one by default. If the user wants UGC with a creator demonstrating the desk, change the route and add performance/interaction requirements.

## Shot plan

| Shot | Time | Action | Route | Must preserve |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S01 | 0-3 s | Establish the occupied desk in the small room | Real/reference-driven scene or controlled 3D | Room/product scale |
| S02 | 3-9 s | Show the folding mechanism clearly | Supplied real demonstration or verified Blender geometry | Hinge, clearances, dimensions, actual movement |
| S03 | 9-14 s | Reveal the newly usable floor area | Same room/scene reference | Furniture positions, product state |
| S04 | 14-20 s | Product beauty angle with exact CTA overlay | Accepted asset with controlled camera or qualified generation | Product silhouette, label and approved copy |

Total 20 seconds. Plan source handles separately. A longer generated source is not a longer required edit.

## Example shot prompt: S03

> Use ENV-01 for the apartment layout and PROD-01-FOLDED for the approved folded desk appearance. A restrained lateral camera move reveals the floor area in front of the wall. Keep the room geometry, window and chair positions fixed. Soft daylight from frame left, warm neutral materials. The desk remains fully folded throughout; no additional furniture appears. Silent source; titles and sound will be added in the edit.

Attach the actual references through supported roles. Resolve source duration/resolution/aspect in the live schema and quote. The prompt does not establish that the generator can preserve the product; inspect a representative test before committing more shots.

## Savings recommendation

Reuse the same scene/assets for S01-S03 where possible. Use lower-resolution previews to test camera and mechanism readability. A small label or hinge defect may require a higher-resolution check. Compare an accepted-shot upscale with a higher-quality generation only after verifying the action. Add exact CTA/logo in Resolve, avoiding repeated generation for text.

For several future ads, Blender setup may pay off through reusable geometry. For this one ad, an existing real demonstration might be cheaper and more accurate. Compare actual asset availability and setup effort before choosing.

## Review

Check product truth first, then aesthetics. Do not state a specific amount of space saved unless measured/sourced. Verify labels, mechanism contact and layout across the cut. Assess voice/music clarity if added. Test the final crop and exact CTA at phone size. Stop at the authorized attempt cap and report remaining defects rather than hiding them with an inaccurate crop.
